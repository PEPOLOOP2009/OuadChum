Our Philosophy

OuadChum Philosophy: Business to Help

'Business to Help' means establishing a business to empower communities that already have their own resources and government support, ensuring they utilize them with maximum efficiency.

OuadChum team focuses on collaborating with universities and state funding agencies, which possess extensive community networks and research. We want OuadChum to bridge this knowledge with real-world practice, using Chumphon as our pilot area.

Our core framework is Holistic Area Based Community Development (HAB), striving to build a sustainable model at the sub-district level.
